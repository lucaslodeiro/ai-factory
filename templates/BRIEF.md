The human approves this brief instead of reading the SPEC. Aim for 5000 characters; a longer complete brief is still valid. The SPEC targets 25000 characters; a longer complete SPEC is still valid.

## Decisions for you
At most ten, most irreversible first. Write "None." when there are none.

**D1. <the question>**
Recommended: <option>. If this is wrong: <consequence>. Alternative: <option>.

## Solution
At most five lines, in product terms: what changes for whom.

## Acceptance criteria
One line per criterion, same IDs as the structured acceptanceCriteria.

## Assumed without asking
Operational choices you made yourself. The human can correct them; they do not block approval.

## Stories
"None." when the issue is delivered whole. Otherwise one line per story: key, title, the criteria it owns, the stories it waits for, and its risk and verification depth. Splitting is a decision above, with the reason the parts are independent.

## Assessment
Complexity, risk, verification depth and UX impact (none / minor / significant), one line each.
